# Candidate decoration item icons 103–111 — V2 simplified

Built-in ImageGen is called independently for every icon. Each source uses a removable flat chroma-key background. The transparent subject is reduced onto a 512×512 canvas and checked again at 64×64.

## Shared V2 direction

- Game UI item icon designed to remain instantly readable at 64–80 px.
- Exactly one bold, compact subject silhouette; at most one large effect symbol.
- Two or three principal subject colors only.
- Thick warm dark-brown outline, very sparse interior lines, large flat color regions.
- Only two or three cel-shading values; almost no texture and very few broad highlights.
- Source subject centered and occupying about 85–92% of the color-key source canvas so it can be reduced onto the final canvas.
- Final subject target: about 65–75% of the 512 canvas, with at least 40 px transparent padding on every side.
- No small accessory clutter, complex ornament, repeated tiny details, multiple panels, text, letters, numbers, pseudo-text, complete background, floor plane, border, cast/contact shadow, reflection, particles, watermark, logo, or anthropomorphic features.
- Chroma background is a single perfectly uniform color with no gradient, vignette, texture, lighting variation, or shadow. Subject edges are crisp and completely separated from it.
