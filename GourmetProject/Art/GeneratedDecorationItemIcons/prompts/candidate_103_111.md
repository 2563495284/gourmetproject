# Candidate decoration item icons 103–111

Built-in ImageGen was used once per asset. All source images use a removable flat chroma-key background and are post-processed locally to transparent 512×512 PNGs.

## Shared art direction

- Use case: stylized-concept
- Asset type: game UI item icon
- One opaque restaurant tabletop, wall-mounted, or floor-standing prop only
- Square composition, centered subject filling roughly 70–85% of the canvas, generous clean padding, readable at 70–80 px
- Friendly polished restaurant-game illustration, three-quarter view where useful
- Thick warm dark-brown outer contour and restrained dark-brown interior linework
- Two or three layers of clean cel shading with soft painted highlights
- Cream, warm gold, coral, and natural wood, accented by peacock blue or teal
- No text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, background scene, border, cast shadow, contact shadow, reflection, floor plane, particles, glow, or anthropomorphic features
- The chroma-key background must be perfectly flat, uniform, and uninterrupted with no gradient, texture, vignette, lighting variation, or shadow. Keep the subject fully separated from it with crisp antialiased edges.
