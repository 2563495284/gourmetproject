## 统一规格

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: simplified game UI icon for a restaurant-decoration item, designed to remain instantly recognizable at 64–80 px
Style/medium: bold flat 2D game-icon illustration; thick warm near-black outline; very large clean color blocks; only two or three main colors; two or three simple cel-shaded value steps; almost no texture and only one restrained highlight.
Composition/framing: exactly one strong-silhouette subject, centered, near-front or very slight top-down three-quarter view. The subject occupies about 65–75% of the square canvas and is surrounded by at least 40 px of clear final transparent padding. At most one large integral effect symbol is allowed.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid chroma-key color for local background removal, completely uniform with no gradient, lighting variation, texture, floor plane, shadow, or reflection.
Constraints: prioritize iconographic clarity over object realism. No small loose props, tiny ingredients, complex ornament, patterned surfaces, multiple panels, background scene, cast shadow, contact shadow, glow, particles, words, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, people, faces, eyes, mouth, limbs, mascot traits, or anthropomorphism. No more than four meaningful interior shapes. Keep every edge crisp and fully inside the canvas.
```
