Primary request: {逐项实际主体要求}
Style/medium: cohesive 2D hand-painted casual restaurant game art; warm deep-brown outlines; rounded readable silhouette; two to three cel-shading steps; subtle paper-grain brush texture; match the approved references, not glossy 3D.
Composition/framing: isolated single compact object or tightly unified object cluster, centered, slight top-down three-quarter view, about 70% of a square canvas, generous padding, readable at 80 px.
Color palette: {逐项实际主色}
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid {色键} chroma-key background.
Constraints: background is exactly one uniform {色键} color with no gradients, texture, lighting variation, floor plane, reflection, cast shadow, contact shadow, or halo. Keep every part fully separated from the background with crisp clean edges. Do not use {色键} anywhere in the subject. No people, no scenery, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no drop shadow. Plaques, menus, tickets, tags, boards and panels may use only wordless pictograms.
~~~

## 后处理与校验

- 每个色键源图先用技能自带 remove_chroma_key.py 处理，统一使用 auto-key border、soft-matte、transparent-threshold 12、opaque-threshold 220、despill。
- 洋红色键图中含紫罗兰主体时，官方去色会把主体内部的紫色误识别为溢色。本批次保留官方工具生成的软边和边缘 despill，只对与外部/大型纯色键区域不连通的主体内部恢复源图 RGB 与不透明度，避免紫色变灰或出现透明孔洞。
- 所有成品统一为 512×512 RGBA。
- 四角 alpha 全部为 0。
- 主体 alpha bbox 最大边统一不超过 430 px；最小实际 bbox 为 391 px 最大边，最大为 430 px，未触边。
- 80px 接触表逐项目检通过；没有文字、字母、数字、Logo、背景场景或投影。
- 配色按奶油黄、青绿、天蓝、珊瑚红、紫罗兰、陶瓷白、蜂蜜金轮换。仅明确木制/藤编物件保留少量木色；本批次没有主色过棕、需要仅因配色重做的文件。
